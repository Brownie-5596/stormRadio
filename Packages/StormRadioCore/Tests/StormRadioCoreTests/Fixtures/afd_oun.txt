.LONG TERM...
(Sunday through Wednesday)
Issued at 106 AM CDT Thu Oct 8 2026

On Sunday, the remnants of "Isaias" is expected to be tracking up 
the Mississippi Delta Region.  The circulation from this post-
tropical system will be building a ridge upstream/westward across 
the Southern Plains which will result in hotter daytime temperatures 
for Sunday and Monday. Our area could see highs in the lower to mid 
90s for Sunday & Monday and warmer nights which will be near record-
breaking for this time of the year.  A system moving through the 
main jet stream across the Northeastern U.S. may push the first 
of two cold fronts across our area for next week, with the latter
much stronger at the end of the week. Deterministic models are 
not consistent with the handling of the first front with the 
ECMWF & NBM pushing it into our area Tuesday before stalling out 
across southwest Oklahoma while the GFS stalls it out up in 
Kansas. Based on the first solution, much of our area could see a 
slight cool-down Tuesday from hot to still unseasonably warm with
highs in the lower to mid 80s although our southwest CWA could stay
hot. There is also a 15-20% chance for scattered rain on Tuesday 
with this first potential frontal passage. Rain & Storm chances 
increase becoming more widespread on Wednesday as a large Pacific-
based system starts digging across the U.S. Southwest toward the
Southern Plains.
